# non binary bike club

I joined nbbc last year and it's become a staple of my weekly social life and exercise. Each week we ride a different route together and end at a cool place to hang out. It's a great way to get outside, make friends, and find out about great restaurants/parks/bars/hang-out spots in the city.

This club has slowly turned me from a [fietser](https://bigride.eu/2022/07/04/what-is-a-fietser/) into a *cyclist*. I've found myself going on longer bike rides just for fun and I even participated in the club's 100k populaire. 

The website for the club is [nb.bike](https://nb.bike/).
